Purpose: a standing research and brainstorming desk on how DeepSpace wins business customers. The memos are raw material; the product of this goal is the brief the manager maintains for the CEO on the board (`brief: gtm-b2b`) and the idea bank (`ideas: ideas-b2b`).

Two motions, two buyers (rounds 1 to 5 covered only the first; from round 6 the desk covers both, with the enterprise motion first):
- **Teams (self-serve)**: developer teams and AI-agent-first builders who adopt the SDK themselves: agencies building client apps, SaaS teams adding realtime or collaboration, internal-tool teams, AI-native startups, game and edtech studios. Competitors: Supabase, Firebase, Convex, Liveblocks, PartyKit/raw Durable Objects, the local-first engines, and the AI app builders.
- **Enterprise (services-led, the motion on https://deep.space/enterprise)**: "Built for business. One SDK for your client projects and internal tools. Our team behind you." Custom plans shaped around the customer's apps and usage, hands-on onboarding from the first workflow to production, priority support, platform security controls with SOC 2 in progress (not yet certified). The sales motion is "Start with one workflow": the buyer brings one workflow, both sides agree what success looks like, DeepSpace shows a working demo in 48 hours built around the buyer's own inputs, free with no obligation, then production scope, security and support are shaped together. Reference projects on that page: Unison (natural-language search across about 101,000 private candidate profiles), a sales-operations agent for a confidential client (one agent across browser, Slack and background work, with typed tools, human approvals and durable execution), ToLife (a shared rehearsal workspace with realtime collaboration and AI voice assignment), and the Blitz series (30 apps in 30 days). The customer owns the application: source code, workflows, data model. Built and operated by Eudaimonic Inc. Buyers here are not developers: heads of operations, revenue operations, finance and HR at companies of roughly 50 to 500 people, and agency principals with client projects. Competitors here are agencies and consultancies, internal-tool platforms such as Retool and Superblocks, and forward-deployed-engineering offers.

Stage (every task gets this too): the product is in beta and the team is small. Ideas that need capabilities DeepSpace lacks today (certifications, SSO and SCIM, single-tenant SKUs, a large partner program, a sales team) are "later", not "now"; the desk's job is what a small team can do this quarter. Facts the public pages do not state: no public paid tiers exist, enterprise plans are custom, so memos must treat any price point as a design option, never as a fact. (Yuke: add real numbers here when available, such as team size, current customers and pricing; the desk will use them.)

What DeepSpace is (the framing every task gets; the product-brief task turns the official pages into a short brief that every later task receives under `deps/`):
- "The first app engine — secure, scalable, and production-ready from day one." A full-stack SDK plus hosting on Cloudflare Workers that lets an AI coding agent (or a developer) build, deploy and ship a real-time app from a prompt: auth and role-based permissions, SQLite-backed real-time sync, presence and live cursors, Yjs collaborative editing, channel messaging with reactions and read receipts, file storage, streamed AI chat (Claude, GPT, Cerebras) with tool use, Stripe checkout and paywalls, scheduled and background jobs, and one-command deploys to `<app>.app.space` or a custom domain with TLS. Scaffolds ship Vite + React, a Hono worker, file-based routing, Tailwind v4 and six Durable Object classes. Free to start, paid plans for scale. Positioned against Supabase, Firebase and Convex as "the whole app, not components". There is a hosted builder at https://deep.space, docs at https://docs.deep.space, a dashboard at https://dashboard.deep.space, code at https://github.com/deepdotspace, and a Discord.
- Pages to read (tell the workers to fetch them): https://deep.space, https://deep.space/get-started, https://deep.space/enterprise and the three project pages and the Blitz page it links to ("Explore the project", "Explore the Blitz"), https://docs.deep.space (Get started, Core concepts, Going to production).
